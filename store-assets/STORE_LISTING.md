# Chrome Web Store listing: Lumen Dark

## Description

Late-night study sessions on Lumen no longer have to mean staring into a blazing white screen.

Lumen Dark gives BITS Pilani Digital's Lumen a proper dark theme. D2L's own interface (the header, navigation and course tree) is darkened as a whole, while lesson pages are recoloured element by element, so headings keep their colour, links stay recognisable, and images, diagrams and videos look exactly as your instructor intended.

Features
• Three modes: Dark, Auto (follows your system's light or dark setting) and Light
• Keyboard shortcut to toggle instantly: Alt+Shift+D by default, changeable at chrome://extensions/shortcuts
• No white flash when moving between lessons
• Runs only on lumen.bitspilani-digital.edu.in and nowhere else
• No accounts, no tracking, and no data ever leaves your browser

Found a page that doesn't look right? Use "Report a problem" in the popup, email ojashguptadev@gmail.com, or open an issue at https://github.com/ComputerTech99/lumen-dark/issues

Lumen Dark is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to BITS Pilani or D2L.

## Privacy tab

Single purpose:
Lumen Dark applies a dark colour theme to the BITS Pilani Digital Lumen learning platform (lumen.bitspilani-digital.edu.in). It has no other function.

storage justification:
Used only to remember the user's chosen appearance mode (Dark, Auto or Light) on their own device, so the setting persists between sessions. Nothing else is stored, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Host permission justification:
The extension restyles pages on a single site, lumen.bitspilani-digital.edu.in, which is its entire purpose. The content script must run on that domain (including its embedded lesson frames) to apply the dark theme. It runs on no other website and does not read, collect or transmit any page content or user data.

Remote code: No.
Data usage: tick none of the categories; certify all three disclosures.
