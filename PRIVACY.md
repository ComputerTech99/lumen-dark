# Lumen Dark: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 3 October 2026*

Lumen Dark does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data or browsing information.

**What the extension does.** It runs only on `lumen.bitspilani-digital.edu.in`, where it changes the colours of pages inside your browser. It does not read, record or send any page content anywhere.

**What it stores.** Only your chosen appearance mode (Dark, Auto or Light), kept locally in your browser using Chrome's storage. This setting never leaves your device, and removing the extension deletes it.

**What it doesn't do.** Lumen Dark contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no remote code. All of its code ships inside the extension package and can be read in this repository.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be sent to ojashguptadev@gmail.com.
